Support

Help with renders, purchases and your photos.

Contact
Email netkaktakto@gmail.com with your iPhone model, the room type and look you picked, and what went wrong. Settings, Send feedback fills in the app version.
A redesign failed or looks wrong
Try a brighter photo taken from a corner so floor, walls and windows are all visible. Failed renders do not use up your allowance.
Restore a purchase
Open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore, signed in with the Apple ID that bought it.
Cancel the subscription
Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Refurn.
Where are my saved designs?
In the app's history, and in Photos if you tapped Save. They are not stored on our server.
