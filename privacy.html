Refurn — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Refurn redesigns a photo of your room with AI while keeping the walls, floor and windows as they are. We built it to keep your rooms on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your room photos, the designs Refurn makes, your quiz answers and settings are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server copy of your designs.

Photos you send for a redesign

Only after you agree in the app, the one room photo you choose for a redesign is sent to our own server on Cloudflare, which passes it to an image model (Cloudflare Workers AI) to create the design and returns the picture. The photo is used only to answer that request and is not stored, sold, shared for advertising or used to train models. A random install code (not linked to you) is sent with it so we can apply the weekly limit. Please avoid photographing people.

Purchases and analytics

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We use Adapty to confirm your subscription, measure paywall views, purchases and onboarding steps, and attribute installs from Apple Ads. Adapty receives an anonymous app profile ID, purchase events, your answers to the style quiz and, as part of a normal network request, your IP address (used to determine the country). We don't collect IDFA, use advertising SDKs or sell data.

Photos access

Refurn uses the camera only when you tap Take photo, and asks for add-only access to Photos only to save a design when you tap Save. Choosing a photo uses Apple's picker, which gives Refurn only the photo you select.

Children

Refurn is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting data

Delete any design in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.
