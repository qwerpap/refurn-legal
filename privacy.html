Privacy Policy

In short. Your room photo is sent to our AI service to make the redesign. We don't ask for an account. Purchases run through Apple and a subscription service.

Camera and Photos

The camera starts only when you choose Take photo. Room photos from your library come through Apple's photo picker, so Refurn sees just the picture you select. Refurn asks for permission to add pictures to your library so Save to Photos works; it does not read the rest of your library.

What the AI providers do

The image is processed by the provider named above under its own terms. We send the photo and a fixed instruction written on our server; we do not send your name, email or location. Because the picture may show the inside of your home, avoid including people, documents or screens you would not want processed.

Tracking and ads

No advertising inside the app, no advertising identifier, no tracking across other companies' apps or sites, and no sale of personal data. The IP address passed to Adapty is used for country-level pricing and Apple Ads attribution.
